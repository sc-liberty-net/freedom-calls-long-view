How Members Were Counted

The definition stayed the same from 2016 to 2026: a sustaining member is someone who gave at least $25 in the prior 12 months, or a life member (Bylaws, Art. 4.4(a)). What changed is how staff counted it, twice.

To November 2022: the old count

Read from the membership label on each record in the donor database, first Raiser's Edge, then CiviCRM. The report's column is "By Law Definition of 'Sustaining Member'", and it includes life members.

From December 2022: the current count

A query by the CTO that counts actual gifts across every 365-day period, instead of relying on the label. Executive Director Lainie Huston announced it on June 28, 2023, saying "Hundreds of members have been missing over each of the last 25 months." It still doesn't check when a pledge was signed.

December 2024: a second break

With the move to Zoho, the December 2024 report marks a "Baseline Established Through New System". Sustaining members went from 10,731 in November to 10,569, and life members from 3,286 to 2,975.

Where both counts exist, the current one is higher: by 657 (4.0%) in December 2022, 270 in January 2023, 598 in February, 786 in March and 814 in April. So the old line undercounts by a varying amount, and the step at December 2022 is partly a counting change, not people joining or leaving. A 2022 convention proposal to require a signed pledge as well as dues was not adopted.

Sources: LNC membership reports for December 2022 (p.2), April and May 2023; the May 2023 finance report's explanation; the December 2024 membership report; the 2026 Bylaws, Art. 4.4(a); 2022 convention minutes, p.144.

What The Charts Can And Can't Show

  • For the calls. Members and small gifts both stopped falling for about a year after the calls began, the only flat stretch since 2022. Donors returning after a year away hit the highest counts in the series from late 2023 to mid-2024. The SIC set aside calls marked "Donated" because the people said they had already renewed, and counted none of those renewals.
  • Against. The flat stretch also fits the 2023 card-data loss running its course. The decline after the calls ended is as slow as during them. The steepest fall after mid-2023 came in late 2024, while Freedom Calls was still calling.
  • Open. The renewed fall begins with the August 2024 move of payments and records to Zoho. Whether that move broke recurring charges, as the CiviCRM move did in 2023, is not in the public record.
  • What would settle it. The call lists matched to renewals by member ID, and Zoho's recurring-gift records for August–December 2024. Only the LNC holds them.
Sources and method
  • Gifts: the LNC's FEC reports (committee C00255695), most recent version of each, placed in the quarter its coverage starts. Election-year October–December reports are combined into the fourth quarter.
  • Members: each month's figure as first reported in the LNC's monthly reports. Later reports twice slipped a column and misdated months; this page uses each month's own report.
  • Donors: the LNC's FEC Schedule A rows for line 11(a)(i), January 2021 onward, matched by name and ZIP code. Counts only; no donor is named.
  • Rolling totals and index values are worked out on this page from the source rows; Sources shows each formula.
