How Members Were Counted

The definition stayed the same from 2015 to 2026. A sustaining member is a member of the Party, someone who has "certified in writing" that they oppose the initiation of force (Bylaws, Art. 4.1), who gave at least $25 in the prior 12 months, or a life member (Art. 4.4). The membership reports give the same definition in January 2015, then as Art. 5.3 (January 2015 Report), and in December 2022 (December 2022 Report, P.2). What changed is how staff counted it, twice.

To November 2022: The Old Count

Read from the membership label on each record in the donor database (May 2023 Report, P.1). Every old-count month plotted here, to November 2022, came from Raiser's Edge, which the LNC left at the start of 2023 (January 2023 Finance Report, P.1). CiviCRM's first reports, January to April 2023, kept the old count (April 2023 Report, P.1); this page plots the restated current count for those months instead. The report's column is "By Law Definition of 'Sustaining Member'", and it includes life members (December 2022 Report, P.2).

From December 2022: The Current Count

A query by the CTO that counts actual gifts across every 365-day period, instead of relying on the label (May 2023 Report, P.1). Executive Director Lainie Huston announced it on June 28, 2023 (List Post), saying "Hundreds of members have been missing over each of the last 25 months." (P.2). It still doesn't check when a pledge was signed (P.2).

December 2024: A Second Break

With the move to Zoho, the December 2024 report marks a "Baseline Established Through New System" (December 2024 Report, P.1). Sustaining members went from 10,731 in November (November 2024 Report, P.1) to 10,569, and life members from 3,286 to 2,975 (December 2024 Report, P.1).

Where both counts exist, the current one is higher: by 657 (4.0%) in December 2022, 270 in January 2023, 598 in February, 786 in March and 814 in April (old count: April 2023 Report, P.1; current count: May 2023 Report, P.1). So the old line undercounts by a varying amount, and the step at December 2022 is partly a counting change, not people joining or leaving. A 2022 convention proposal, Proposal F, would have relabelled the same groups as "signatory" and "dues-paying" members, with a sustaining member a "Signatory member who is also dues paying"; the argument against it was that "This is a non-substantive change" (2022 Convention Minutes, P.144). The 2026 Bylaws keep the old wording (2026 Bylaws, Art. 4.4).

Lists, Texts & Call Length

What the record says about the callers' lists, the texts they sent, and how short calls were counted. Each item is marked Record (a document or log), Claim (someone's statement) or Inference (this page's reading). The SIC's authors are interested parties, so its readings are Claims; its Kixie tables are Record.

  • Stale Lists In Spring 2024 (Claim). According to Kennedy, Director of Fundraising Luke Troxell "generated and uploaded lists in Kixie for everyone making calls" until he left on March 24, 2024. "It did not appear as if additional lists had been generated or uploaded since his departure," and by June Freedom Calls "had not been provided updated call lists in a few months" (SIC, June 9, 2025 Minutes, P.47).
  • New Lists Later In 2024 (Claim). Staff told the board that Kixie was now linked to the Zoho records on August 24 (LNC Meeting, August 24, 2024, 0:51:10). By October 6 the callers had project-based and expiring-member lists (LNC Meeting, October 6, 2024, 0:08:33), and on December 7 calls before a membership expires were being automated (LNC Meeting, December 7, 2024, 0:48:18). No upload log is public. One list is certain: 361 "calls and SMS messages" to California contacts on November 20–23 (SIC, P.68). A September 2024 meeting between the contractor Michael Heise and Freedom Calls never took place (SIC, P.48), and list updates "were routed through a small number of individuals" (June 9, 2025 Minutes, P.101).
  • Calls Fell After The New Lists (Record; Weekly Rates Are Inference). Calls ran at about 460 a week from February 4 to May 25, 2024, the stale-list months; about 325 a week to June 29; about 210 a week from July 28 to August 31; about 135 a week in September; and about 55 a week from October 6 to November 2. No table is published for June 30 – July 27 (SIC Kixie Tables, P.60–61; weekly rates are this page's division). So stale lists don't explain the low numbers; calling fell after staff reported new lists (Contra SIC Report, Finding 35).
  • Short Calls (Record & Claim). The SIC measured "Call Duration", "which is the duration from the start of the phone ring to the end of the call." (SIC, P.59). It found "Over 6,000 calls had a call duration of under 30 seconds" and read many as dead-dialing, while saying "The agents are unreachable, so we cannot know what happened" (SIC, P.70). Kixie's own 3-minute rule is for reports only: calls longer than the setting "will count as a connected call", and "The default amount is 180 seconds (3 minutes)" (Kixie, Setting Connection Duration, May 8, 2024). Shorter calls are still logged, and recording is on by default (Kixie, Account Settings, 2025). No one listened to the recordings (Contra SIC Report, Finding 36).
  • Texts (Record & Claim). The SIC's table counts 9,057 texts by account, with no dates: "Peter" 5,680, "Angie" 716, "Ella" 620, FreedomCalls One 516 and FreedomCalls Two 1,525 (SIC, P.64; read from an image table). They were templates: "Custom SMS templates were sent to a fair number of the prospects called, but not all." (P.64). None of them are in the call counts above. The SIC finds that the agents "were misrepresenting themselves as Austin Padgett, Luke Troxell, and Matthew Butts for months via text" before the FreedomCalls accounts began in August and September 2024 (SIC, P.66; Contra SIC Report, Finding 37).
  • Before The Calls (Record). The fall from 2022 began before any calls: the move off Raiser's Edge lost recurring card gifts (Donor Drop-Off, Members & Monthly Pledgers Around The Switch; How The Migration Unfolded), and convention money inflated 2022 (Convention Money In The 2022 Total). The calls audit used Zoho data, but CiviCRM took the gifts for about half the calls (Contra SIC Report, Finding 28).

Quotes checked against the saved texts: the June 9, 2025 minutes, the LNC meeting transcripts, and Kixie's help pages (saved November 20, 2026).

What The Charts Can & Can't Show

  • For The Calls. From September 2023 to August 2024, members stayed between 11,892 and 12,747, and rolling 12-month unitemized gifts held at $699k–$726k through June 2024 (membership reports; FEC Filings, 2023–24). The only other flat stretch since 2022 is 2026, with members between 8,816 and 8,989 from January to October (membership reports), after the calls had ended. Donors returning after a year away peaked at 124 in the fourth quarter of 2023 (FEC Schedule A, Line 11(a)(i)); the count can only look back to 2021, so 2022's returning donors are undercounted. The SIC called the calls' "Donated" label misleading: it appears to mean the person said they had already renewed, not that the call brought in a gift (SIC Report P.47; LNC Minutes, June 9, 2025, P.63).
  • Against. The flat stretch also fits the 2023 card-data loss running its course (LNC Meeting, March 11, 2023, 1:57:28; January 2023 Finance Report, P.1). The decline was faster in the last year of calls than in the year after: members fell 15.9% from January 2024 to January 2025 and 13.3% from January 2025 to January 2026, and rolling 12-month unitemized gifts fell 33% from March 2024 to March 2025 and 18% the next year (membership reports; FEC reports). The steepest fall after mid-2023 came in late 2024, while Freedom Calls was still calling (membership reports; SIC Report P.42).
  • Open. The renewed fall, in unitemized gifts from the second quarter of 2024 and in members from mid-2024, coincides with the move to Zoho. The finance reports show only the payments side of that move: the undeposited-funds account named "civi" in July 2024 is "Zoho / Checks" in August (July 2024 Finance Report, P.8; August 2024 Finance Report, P.8). Whether that move broke recurring charges, as the CiviCRM move did in 2023 (LNC Meeting, March 11, 2023, 1:57:28), is not in the public record.
  • What Would Settle It. The call lists matched to renewals by member ID, and Zoho's recurring-gift records for August–December 2024. Only the LNC holds them.
